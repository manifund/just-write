# Just Write

Type: idea
Created by: Austin Chen
Created time: October 24, 2026 10:22 AM
Priority: P1
Status: Alive

*4-week online writing club. publish a snippet every day, full essay every week. $1k deposit required, refunded if you succeed. [justwrite.club](http://justwrite.club)*

### Thesis

- Everyone has a great essay in them.
    - We especially want to hear from people who aren’t “fulltime writers”
    - You shouldn’t have to quit your job to write
- Social and financial commitments can help people achieve great things, become more of the person they want to be

### Structure

- Oct 24: Applications open. Rolling admissions.
- Oct 30: Applications
- Nov 1: Kickoff
- Each week:
    - **Shortform**: Publish 100+ words each day
        - On X, LW, personal blog, etc
        - Deadline: noon PT. One “grace day” each week.
    - **Longform**: Publish 1000+ word essay each week
        - Aiming for a timeless, high quality essay
        - Can reuse words from previous shortforms
        - Deadline: noon PT the following Monday
    - **In person**: weekly writing circles in SF (optional)
        - Tentatively on Saturdays (Nov 7/14/21/28)
- Tues Dec 1: Wrap party (?)
    - (Awkwardly supposed to be in Boston for this)

### FAQ

**Q: Do you have to write about anything in particular?**

No! But we encourage you to work on essays that could be important, timeless, ambitious. Something you’ll be proud of, a decade from now.

You could write about your work. About your love. About a message you desperately want the world to hear. About a subject you’re just wading into. Anything, as long as you are crafting with care.

**Q: Is this a remote or in-person program?**

Remote by default, but with an optional in-person weekly meetups in San Francisco.

We’ll have a Discord server for people to chat & get feedback; and weekly Saturday afternoon meetups at Mox dedicated to writing.

**Q: Beyond the writing requirements, what else does Just Write offer?**

TBD, but probably things like:

- Weekly challenges (eg debate another clubmate; write a profile on a close friend)
- Peer feedback (eg review-for-review trade)
- Online community of other writers

### Open design questions

- Does the daily snippet posting make sense?
    - Idea is to: make sure people are doing a bit of writing each day; encourage people to practice posting catchy snippets
    - But: maybe that’s antithetical to writing a good essay. which is the overall goal of the program
- What is the admission bar?
    - No admission bar?
        - Maybe the $1k deposit is enough to screen for good people
    - Medium admission bar?
    - High admission bar?
    - Invite only?
        - I do kind of feel like I could fill out a pretty good cohort just inviting friends and writers I like
        - But idk maybe that’s too elitist of me
    - Vishal on gatekeeping Inkhaven 3: [https://x.com/koreindian/status/2111477031309627638](https://x.com/koreindian/status/2111477031309627638)
    - How much do lower quality participants detract from the participation experience?
        - In an online program, might be okay to be much much more hitsbased. More nanowrimo than inkhaven?
- Is there an ideal number of participants?
    - 10?
    - 30?
        - This is my current guess for the first cohort
    - 100?
- What are the official feedback mechanisms?
    - None required
    - Peer feedback
    - AI feedback
    - Coach feedback
        - How much of a value add are dedicated writing coaches?
- SF IRL meetups: on weekdays vs weekends?
    - Saturday seems like an okay default but maybe cuts into things other people want to do
    - Morning vs afternoon?
- What are ways to make this appealing to:
    - Cool writer people like <any past Manifest speaker>

    - rationalist tumblrfolk
- How do we orient to, position compared to Inkhaven 3?
    - Kind of want “friendly competition” vibe, like, which set of essays produced are better or get more views etc
    - Is it rude to offer people to join both programs? Requirements here are (kind of) a subset of Inkhaven
    - Would love to have participants join Inkhaven fair
    - See also [https://conq.blog/2026/09/20/eurohaven/](https://conq.blog/2026/09/20/eurohaven/)
- Design challenges during the week? Eg
    - Themes, cause areas,
    - Formats
        - Essay/Thinkpiece
        - Fiction
        - Research paper, research notes
        - Interview/podcast
        - platform-optimized (X vs Substack vs LW etc)

        - Debate
        - Adversarial collab
        - matching “great writer” w/ “domain expert”
